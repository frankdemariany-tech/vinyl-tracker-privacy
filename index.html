Vinyl Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 6, 2026

Vinyl Tracker is an internal inventory management application operated by G T Goods, Inc. This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, used, and protected when Vinyl Tracker connects to an authorized eBay account.

Information We Access

Vinyl Tracker may access eBay account information that the authorized user has granted permission to access through eBay's OAuth authorization process. This may include listing information, inventory information, item identifiers, titles, quantities, and other seller-owned data necessary to support inventory management and listing synchronization.

How We Use Information

Information accessed through eBay is used only to support the operation of Vinyl Tracker, including inventory reconciliation, listing identification, stock monitoring, and related seller inventory management functions.

Vinyl Tracker does not sell, rent, or share eBay account data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Authentication and Passwords

Vinyl Tracker does not collect or store eBay account passwords. Authentication is handled directly through eBay's OAuth authorization system.

Data Storage

Inventory and listing information may be stored locally or within systems controlled by G T Goods, Inc. for the purpose of maintaining inventory history, listing mappings, and application functionality.

Data Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect data accessed through Vinyl Tracker. Access is limited to authorized use for the operation of the application.

Data Retention and Removal

Data is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for inventory management and application operation. Authorized users may revoke eBay access through their eBay account settings or developer authorization controls.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Vinyl Tracker's functionality evolves. Any updates will be published on this page.
